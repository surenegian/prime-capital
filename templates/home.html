Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Download it and start. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. If you turn sync on, it is backed up to your own private iCloud, which we never see.

What happens to a scanned receipt?

It is read on your iPhone to fill in the amount, date and category. It is never stored.

Can I set a budget?

Yes. Give any category a monthly spending limit. If an entry takes you over it, the app tells you and lets you raise the limit for just this month or for good.

Can I bring my bank's data in?

Yes. Import an OFX, QFX or CSV export and match its columns in a few taps.

Can I keep a backup myself?

Yes. Save a local copy from Data and Reports and restore from it later. It includes every profile.

Can I keep personal and business apart?

Yes. Add a profile for each, with its own accounts, categories, budgets and look. Switch from the top of the dashboard, and pick which one opens first.

Which languages?

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian and Armenian.
