Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

Effective: September 26, 2026 Applies to: Prime Capital for iOS

The short version

  • There's no server. Prime Capital has no backend of any kind — nothing you enter is ever sent to us.
  • There's no account. No sign-up, no login, no email address collected.
  • Your data stays on your device, and — only if you choose to enable it — syncs through your own private iCloud account. We never see it.
  • No analytics, no ad tracking, no third-party SDKs. The one outside network request is one you trigger yourself: an optional currency exchange-rate lookup that sends only two currency codes, nothing about you.
  • The only way information ever reaches us is if you choose to email us through the app's "Send Feedback" button.

Data we don't collect

Prime Capital does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no server for it to go to — every balance, transaction, and category you enter is processed entirely on your iPhone.

We don't use analytics tools, crash reporters, advertising networks, or any third-party software development kit. Nothing about how you use the app is measured, logged, or reported to us or anyone else.

What's stored, and where

Your accounts, transactions, categories, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database framework (SwiftData). This data is sandboxed to the app by iOS itself — no other app can read it.

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, Prime Capital can optionally back up and sync your data through your own private CloudKit database — the same private storage space Apple gives every iCloud account for apps you use. This is what lets deleting and reinstalling the app, or using it on a second device, restore your data.

This sync happens directly between your devices and your own iCloud account. It does not pass through any server we operate, because we don't operate one.

Exchange rate lookups

If you set up an account in a different currency, you can tap "Fetch Rate" to look up that day's exchange rate instead of typing it in yourself. This is the only network request Prime Capital makes to anything other than your own iCloud, and it only happens when you tap that button — never automatically or in the background.

The request goes to a public, third-party currency-data service (fawazahmed0/currency-api, delivered via the jsDelivr content delivery network, with a Cloudflare Pages mirror as a fallback) and asks only for that day's rate between two currency codes, for example "EUR to USD." It never includes your name, your balances, your transactions, or anything else about you — it's the same request anyone in the world could make. If you'd rather not make this request at all, you can always type a rate in by hand instead.

Face ID & app lock

If you turn on Face ID or set a PIN to lock the app, that authentication is handled entirely by iOS on your device. We never receive your face data — Apple's Face ID system never exposes it to any app, including this one. A PIN, if you set one, is stored as a one-way hash in the device Keychain, never as readable digits, and never leaves your device except through your own iCloud Keychain, if you have that enabled.

Home Screen widget

If you add the Prime Capital widget to your Home Screen, the app shares a small snapshot of your balances and categories with it using Apple's App Group feature — a private, on-device handoff between the app and its widget. This never leaves your device.

Receipt scanning

The Add Transaction screen can read a receipt for you — from your camera, a photo, or a file. The image is read entirely on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition to fill in the amount and date. It is never uploaded, never saved, and is discarded as soon as it has been read.

If you contact us

The app's "Send Feedback" button opens your device's Mail app, pre-addressed to us, optionally including your app version and iOS version to help us understand a bug report. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself. Whatever you write, along with your email address, is handled the same as any other email you send — we don't add it to a mailing list or share it further.

Children's privacy

Prime Capital is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — this follows directly from the app having no data collection at all, regardless of who's using it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Given how little the app actually does with data, we don't expect that to happen often.
